Planet artwork credits — patrykopilka.me
======================================

Planet maps adapted from Solar System Scope (INOVE):
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source maps: Earth Day Map and Earth Clouds, Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune.
Changes: Earth day map composited with its cloud layer, maps resized and
reencoded as WebP, then presented with CSS shading and decorative Saturn rings.

Visual reference: Solar CSSystem by Rob DiMarzo
https://codepen.io/robdimarzo/pen/LMOLer
The homepage uses original integration, shading, and rings, not the Pen's
code or its externally hosted textures. Spin speeds, tilts, scale and colors
are illustrative, not a scientific visualization.
